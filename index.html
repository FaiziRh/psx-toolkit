📈 PSX Investor Toolkit

Prototype of four tools for Pakistan Stock Exchange investors.

Demo data: stock prices, dividends and ratios below are illustrative placeholders, not real market data. The PKRV curve is the 20 Oct 2026 snapshot from Mettis. Tax rates are editable defaults; verify with your broker or tax adviser. Not investment advice.

Dividend & Shariah screener

Shariah (ratios) is a simplified debt and interest-income test. The KMI index column shows official membership once you load real data, and should be the final reference.

CGT & portfolio tracker

Buys before 1 Jul 2013 are treated as 0% CGT. The Jul 2022–Jun 2024 band depends on holding period, so set its rate manually. Estimated CGT assumes you sell today at the current price.

Yield comparison vs PKRV

PKRV snapshot dated 20-10-2026. Tenors up to 1Y track T-bills; 2Y and longer track PIBs. Gross comparison: dividends are taxed at the WHT rate above, and a stock’s dividend can be cut, while government paper has no equity risk.

Corporate announcement parser

Paste an announcement’s text. Fields are extracted with pattern matching; always check the result against the original notice.
